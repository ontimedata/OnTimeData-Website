Standards

The checklist before you trust an AI with your pipeline

The standards every AI system should meet before it handles your pipeline.

Does the AI actually sound human?

If your prospect can tell it's a bot in two messages, you've lost them. Our AI texts like the rep you wish you could clone.

Is it genuinely done for you?

Built, trained, and live in under seven days. You don't touch a prompt. You don't manage a workflow. You just watch bookings show up.

Do you only pay when it works?

Zero upfront. Run a free trial on real traffic and pay only for booked appointments.

Can it handle rapid-fire texters?

When a prospect fires off three messages in ten seconds, the AI reads the room, waits, then responds to the whole thread in one natural reply.

Does it book in the conversation?

No "click this link" awkwardness. The AI proposes times mid-conversation and locks the appointment in.

Does it know when to quit?

When a prospect says no, means it, and isn't coming back, the AI stops. No pestering. No damaged brand.

Where does it actually run?

On AI infrastructure we operate and monitor ourselves. End-to-end encrypted. No fragile, stitched-together no-code tools.

Will it talk to your existing stack?

Custom-coded integrations to whatever CRM, calendar, or pipeline you already run. We adapt to your workflow, not the other way around.
